Ishihara is a typeface with the structure of Braille. Each glyph is the Braille cell for its letter with the dots joined into strokes, so the upper four dots are drawn as one connected shape and the lower dots are joined when a letter has more than one. Joined in this way, a, b, k and l each condense to a single column. Glyphs are anchored to the top of the line and have no baseline, since Braille is never written by hand and leaves no calligraphic strokes to inherit.

Numbers are the first ten letters with a # before them. The punctuation is partly derived from Braille, and W is a special case because it was not in the French alphabet that Braille was designed for. In the second image the pangram "How vexingly quick daft zebras jump" is set three times, in Latin letters, in Braille and in Ishihara, so that each cell sits above the glyph made from it.

Chronograph uses Ishihara as one of its obfuscation modes. The font is a single OpenType file in one weight, drawn in Figma and released under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
